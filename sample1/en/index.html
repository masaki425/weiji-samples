W Eiji: two Eiji Watanabes

Artist

Eiji Watanabe

渡辺英司

Artist. Born in Aichi Prefecture, 1961. The “Garden of the Appellation” series cut from illustrated encyclopedias, titles as wordplay, and projects that make places. Profile

Scientist

Eiji Watanabe

渡辺英治

Neuroscientist. Born in Osaka, 1962. Associate professor at the National Institute for Basic Biology, Okazaki. The brain’s sodium sensor, vision in medaka, optical illusions and deep learning. Profile

Morning Monsters / W Eiji

Considering the ordinary as being non-ordinary
Considering the non-ordinary as something ordinary

Lead text of the flyer (Satsuki Yamamoto)

The same surname, the same given name, the same reading. The artist Eiji Watanabe (born in Aichi, 1961) and the neuroscientist Eiji Watanabe (born in Osaka, 1962), both based in Aichi, met for the first time in December 2024. The story of the encounter

Parallel Timelineパラレル年表

    Born in Aichi Prefecture
  2. 1962

Art Obulist 2025 Eiji Watanabe Exhibition
“Morning Monsters / W Eiji”

Dates
Saturday, November 1 – Sunday, December 7, 2025
Venue
Okura Park: Management Building, Rest Building and courtyard (Obu, Aichi)
Organizer
Art Obulist Executive Committee, City of Obu

The exhibition has ended. The neuroscientist’s exhibits in the Management Building, the artist’s works in the Rest Building, and the paths of the park joining the two.

Exhibition record

Artist Talk “We Two are Tacticians”対談「ふたりは策士」

ArtistIt’s like climbing a mountain from opposite ends, from places with nothing in common, and at the very top meeting for a moment and passing each other.

ScientistYes, yes, meeting for an instant, like that. That’s what it feels like.

The only occasion in the exhibition when the two stood in the same place and talked. Rest Building, Okura Park, 2025.11.22 sat. Moderator: Satsuki Yamamoto.
