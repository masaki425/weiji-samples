Eiji × Eiji二人の経歴

Eiji Watanabe (artist) and Eiji Watanabe (neuroscientist)

The same surname, the same given name, the same reading, born a year apart.

Eiji Watanabe (artist)渡辺英司

Artist美術家

Known for the “Garden of the Appellation” series of plants and fish cut out of illustrated encyclopedias, for titles that play on words, and for making places: Star Gallery, Art Obulist, Jingu339. He works in studios in Nagoya and Agui.

Profile of the artist

Eiji Watanabe (neuroscientist)渡辺英治

Scientist神経科学者

He studies the brain’s sodium-level sensor Nax, vision and social behavior in medaka, and optical illusions and deep learning, and presents optical illusions of his own. His books include a beginner’s guide to deep learning for neuroscientists (Yodosha, 2025).
