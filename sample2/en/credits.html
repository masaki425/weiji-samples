The English texts of the catalogue (Andreas Stuhlmann, Adam Simmonds) are used for the event data, the lists of works, the biographies, the question box, the related events, the acknowledgments and the quotations from the contributed texts. Everything else on the English pages, including the About page, the timeline, the chapters and excerpts of the talk, the news, the captions and the interface, is a draft translation made for this site and awaiting review. About this siteこのサイトについて

Site
Masaki Watabe (texts and prototype sample1, September 2026)
Prototype
sample2 (September 2026). A white background, with vertical text limited to the artist’s introduction and the introduction to his works in the Japanese version. The English version is horizontal throughout. No line drawings are used.

A prototype that collects the record of the exhibition after its close and the continuing activities of the two Eiji Watanabes. Names, headings, numbers, labels and thin rules use blue for the artist, vermilion for the neuroscientist and green for what they share; the body text is black. The content has not yet been checked by the Executive Committee and the two Eijis, and some photo credits and external links are still being confirmed.
