Excerpts抜粋

From the transcript in the catalogue. Long passages are cut with (…). The speakers are colored: Artist blue, Scientist vermilion, the moderator green. The English is a draft translation.

03 An exhibition made without meeting

ArtistSo this is only about the fourth time we’ve met.

ScientistWe haven’t met since the installation began, and I haven’t seen his works. I did my part and he did his. The one thing you could call a joint work is the “Funhouse,” where balls roll uphill: my proposal, built by him. And I never saw it being made either. We worked completely independently, and only at the opening did we each grasp the whole of the other’s display.

04 Toilet paper, left and right

ScientistThere’s a strange thing over here: toilet paper. On the left, if you keep unrolling the toilet paper, it turns (folded) into tissue paper.

Artist“Toilet Tissue Paper.”

ScientistRight, and that one is an artwork. The one on the right: as you unroll the toilet paper, the roll gets smaller and at the same time the hole in the middle starts to look bigger. The same tissue paper, but this one is an optical illusion. So the right is science and the left is art. The same material. (…) But when I looked at all the works on the art side and saw that fish piece (“Sea of Eden”), I thought, could it be? Maybe there is some place where the roots coincide. After that I started adding a few pieces of my own.

05 Climbing the mountain from both sides

ArtistIt’s like climbing a mountain from opposite ends, from places with nothing in common, and at the very top meeting for a moment and passing each other.

ScientistYes, yes, meeting for an instant, like that. That’s what it feels like.

ArtistNot that we stop to confirm, “that’s it.” Each of us has come there on our own, so it’s parallel.

ScientistA little nod, that sort of thing.

ArtistWe don’t intersect. We don’t intersect, but we reach the same summit, pass each other, and go down the other side. That’s the feeling.

YamamotoAnd from the summit on, each of you goes down to the foot alone again.

ArtistParallel, right now, means we face the same way and simply never meet. In a sense, parallel means being together forever, as far as direction goes.

It seems my papers smell strongly of me. So they may turn out to be somewhat artistic papers.

Eiji Watanabe (neuroscientist), 08 Science bound to truth, art free

10 “‘What is X?’ is what X is”

ScientistTo this day, when someone asks me “What is art?” I can’t answer. As for “What is science?”, this time a child asked, “Why do you try to know what you don’t know?” (…) That one I can answer. Art I still can’t.

ArtistThat’s the structure of the question. Take painting: “What is painting?” has been asked for centuries. (…) It’s simple: “‘What is painting?’ is what painting is.” You just turn the question into a statement.

ScientistThen maybe I should make all my question-box answers that.
