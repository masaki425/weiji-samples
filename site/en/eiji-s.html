Profile経歴

Eiji Watanabe渡辺英治

Scientist神経科学者

Born in 1962 in the old downtown of Osaka. Graduated from the Department of Biology, Faculty of Science, Osaka University, in 1986, and received a Ph.D. in engineering from the university’s Department of Biotechnology, School of Engineering Science, in 1991. After working as a researcher at the Aichi Institute for Developmental Research, he moved to the National Institute for Basic Biology in Okazaki in 1995, and has been an associate professor of its Laboratory of Neurophysiology since 1998.

His research covers the brain’s sodium-level sensor, the Nax channel; vision and behavior in medaka fish; and optical illusions and deep learning. He also presents optical illusions of his own, among them “Morning Monster” (2020, with Lana Sinapayen) and “Sea of Eden, Fish Liberated from Optical Illusion,” invented during this exhibition.

Threads研究の柱

The brain’s sodium sensor
Sensing of sodium levels in body fluids by the Nax channel, and the control of salt intake (2002 Nat Neurosci, 2007 Neuron)
Vision and behavior in medaka
Predation induced by pink-noise motion (2012 Sci Rep); attraction to biological motion (2014 Anim Cogn)
Optical illusions and AI
Deep neural networks trained for prediction perceive the “rotating snakes” illusion as humans do (2018 Front Psychol, 2022 Sci Rep)
Optical illusions of his own
Watanabe illusion, Okazaki Genkikan Illusion, Morning Monster (2020), Sea of Eden, Fish Liberated from Optical Illusion (2025)

His research and art activities were introduced on the YouTube channel “Hakase to Dokeshi” (The Doctor and the Clown).
