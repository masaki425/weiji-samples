About this siteこのサイトについて

The production website (site) records the exhibition after its close and gathers the continuing activities of the two Eiji Watanabes. It is based on sample3, “Parallel”, which is retained as a record. The whole Home page is a single timeline, with the artist on the left, the scientist on the right and the years on the central axis. The design follows the catalogue and the flyer: three colors, blue for the artist, vermilion for the scientist and green for what they share; the artist’s side is set vertically in Japanese. The content has not yet been checked by the executive committee or the two Eijis, and some photo credits and external links are still being confirmed.
