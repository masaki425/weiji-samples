W Eiji

Two Eiji Watanabes. Two parallel lives that cross in December 2024.

Eiji Watanabe

Artist美術家

Born in Aichi Prefecture in 1961. The “Garden of the Appellation” series cut from illustrated encyclopedias, titles as wordplay, and projects that make places.

Profile

Eiji Watanabe

Scientist神経科学者

Born in the old downtown of Osaka in 1962. Associate professor at the National Institute for Basic Biology, Okazaki. The brain’s sodium sensor, vision in medaka, optical illusions and deep learning.
