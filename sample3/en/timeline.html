Parallel Timelineパラレル年表

From 1961 / 1962 to the meeting in December 20241961 / 1962 から、2024年12月の出会いまで

On the left, the artist Eiji Watanabe; on the right, the neuroscientist Eiji Watanabe. Never crossing, they draw closer and meet in 2024. What the two share is placed in the middle.

Eiji Watanabe (artist)Eiji Watanabe (neuroscientist)Shared

  1. 1961
    Born in Aichi Prefecture
  2. 1962
    Born in the old downtown of Osaka
  3. 1985
    Graduated from the sculpture course, Aichi Prefectural University of Fine Arts and Music
  4. 1986
    Graduated from the Department of Biology, Faculty of Science, Osaka University
  5. 1989
    First solo exhibition (Stegosaurus Studio, Nagoya; to 1993)
    First paper (Neuroscience)
  6. 1991
    Shown in the 10th “Parallelism in Art”
    Ph.D. in engineering, Osaka University
  7. 1992
    Researcher at the Aichi Institute for Developmental Research. Moves to Aichi
  8. 1994
    First solo exhibition at Kenji Taki Gallery, “Stroke of Projection”
  9. 1995
    Assistant professor at the National Institute for Basic Biology, Okazaki. The Neuroglycan C paper
  10. 1997
    “Name of a Star,” “Garden of the Appellation 1997”
  11. 1998
    Associate professor at the National Institute for Basic Biology and at the Graduate University for Advanced Studies
  12. 2000
    The paper on the Nav2/NaG channel, which controls salt intake (J Neurosci)
  13. 2001
    First residencies, “Art in the Home” (Yamaguchi 2001, Edinburgh 2002)
    The paper on Nax, the brain’s sodium sensor (Nat Neurosci, 2002). PRESTO researcher (to 2006)
  14. 2004
    To Scotland on an Agency for Cultural Affairs fellowship (to 2005)
  15. 2007
    “All about Laughter” at the Mori Art Museum; “Cycle and Recycle” at the Aichi Prefectural Museum of Art
    The paper on glial Nax (Neuron)
  16. 2009
    “Talking Mirror” (Mugaen, Hekinan); “Butterfly’s-Eye View” (Cairn Gallery, Scotland)
  17. 2010
    Aichi Triennale 2010. Opens Star Gallery (to 2016)
    First paper on optical illusions (with Akiyoshi Kitaoka, Vision Research)
  18. 2013
    Nissan Art Award 2013
  19. c.2015
    “Between Botany and Art,” Aichi Prefectural Museum of Art
    Searching for images of “eijiwatanabe,” the artist finds a researcher with the same name and images of optical illusions
  20. 2016
    Founds Art Obulist; “Hurry! Slowly”
    Produces an art exhibition at the National Institute for Basic Biology (Toshiki Mikami, a former student of the artist, takes part)
  21. 2017
    ARTZUID (Amsterdam)
    The paper on seasonal changes in color vision (Nat Commun)
  22. 2018
    “Dream of Butterfly” (Fuji, to 2019)
    A predictive-learning AI reproduces the “rotating snakes” illusion (Front Psychol). Co-authors a book on ancient history and science
  23. 2020
    “Merman”
    The optical illusion “Morning Monster” (with Lana Sinapayen)
  24. 2022
    “Sea of Eden”
    Head of the AI analysis facility. Illusion patterns extracted from photographs and paintings (Sci Rep)
  25. 2023
    Opens Jingu339 (Nagoya)
  26. 2024
    “The Morning Star”
    In autumn, the artist meets Hidehiko Komatsu at the Okazaemon art festival. In December, first meeting at the National Institute for Basic Biology, Okazaki
  27. 2025
    Tokyo Biennale, ARTZUID 2025
    Publishes a beginner’s guide to deep learning for neuroscientists
    11.1–12.7 Eiji Watanabe Exhibition “Morning Monsters / W Eiji” (Okura Park, Obu)
  28. 2026
    3.28 counter lecture vol.02 “Analyze W Eiji” (Masaki Watabe, Jingu339). 3.31 Catalogue published. 7.17 Video of the talk released

Sources: the biographies in the catalogue, the artist talk “We Two are Tacticians” (2025.11.22), the exhibition report. The timeline will be extended.
