Eiji × Eiji二人の経歴

Eiji Watanabe and Eiji Watanabe渡辺英司と渡辺英治

The same surname, the same given name, the same reading, born a year apart. On the left, the artist Eiji Watanabe; on the right, the neuroscientist Eiji Watanabe. Their two pages share one form; only the photographs differ.

Eiji Watanabe

Artist美術家

Born in Aichi Prefecture in 1961. Graduated from the sculpture course of Aichi Prefectural University of Fine Arts and Music in 1985. In 2004–2005 he stayed in Scotland as an overseas research fellow of the Agency for Cultural Affairs and was a visiting researcher at Edinburgh College of Art. Known for the “Garden of the Appellation” series of plants and fish cut out of illustrated encyclopedias, for titles that play on words, and for making places: Star Gallery, Art Obulist, Jingu339. He works in studios in Nagoya and Agui.

Profile of the artist

Eiji Watanabe

Scientist神経科学者

Born in the old downtown of Osaka in 1962. Graduated from the Department of Biology, Faculty of Science, Osaka University, in 1986, and received a Ph.D. in engineering there in 1991. Associate professor of the Laboratory of Neurophysiology at the National Institute for Basic Biology in Okazaki since 1998, and head of its AI analysis facility since 2022. He studies the brain’s sodium-level sensor Nax, vision and social behavior in medaka, and optical illusions and deep learning, and presents optical illusions of his own. His books include a beginner’s guide to deep learning for neuroscientists (Yodosha, 2025).
