Profile経歴

Eiji Watanabe渡辺英司

Artistアーティスト

Born in Aichi Prefecture in 1961. Graduated from the sculpture course of Aichi Prefectural University of Fine Arts and Music in 1985, and held his first solo exhibition at Stegosaurus Studio, Nagoya, in 1989. Since 1994 he has shown regularly at Kenji Taki Gallery.

He is known for the “Garden of the Appellation” series, in which plants and fish cut out of illustrated encyclopedias are laid out like a garden; for titles that play on words, such as “Butterfly’s-Eye View” and “Sea of Eden”; and for making places: Star Gallery, Art Obulist, Jingu339. In 2004–2005 he stayed in Scotland as an overseas research fellow of the Agency for Cultural Affairs.

He works in studios in Nagoya and in Agui, Aichi.
