Translation
The English texts of the catalogue (Andreas Stuhlmann, Adam Simmonds) are used for the event data, the lists of works, the biographies, the question box, the related events, the acknowledgments and the quotations from the contributed texts. Everything else on the English pages, including the About page, the timeline, the chapters and excerpts of the talk, the news, the captions and the interface, is a draft translation made for this site and awaiting review. Names are given in Western order (Eiji Watanabe); the catalogue’s colophon uses Japanese order

写真と引用についてPhotos and quotations

  • The documentary photographs are by Kenji Aoki and may not be reproduced. The same applies to the photographs provided by the Executive Committee and the exhibitors.
  • Akiyoshi Kitaoka’s illusion images (rotating snakes, “big car” illusion) are not shown on this site. See the creator’s website.
  • The names of the questioners in the question box have been withheld.
  • The talk video is only embedded from, and linked to, the official Art Obulist channel. Quotations of the speakers follow the transcript in the catalogue; their English wording on this site is a draft.
  • Quotations from the contributed texts are limited to one sentence each, with the catalogue page given. The full texts are in the catalogue.

このサイトについてAbout this site

Site
Masaki Watabe (texts and prototype sample1, September 2026)
Prototype
sample4 (September 2026). It uses the catalogue’s chapter colors, with body text in one column and wide photographs. Color panels are used only for the profile chapter openings; the artist’s name and Japanese introduction are vertical. The English version is horizontal. The cover carries a mirrored title and line drawings.

A prototype that collects the record of the exhibition after its close and the continuing activities of the two Eiji Watanabes. Body text, names, headings, numbers, labels and thin rules use blue for the artist, vermilion for the neuroscientist and green for what they share; links are black. The content has not yet been checked by the Executive Committee and the two Eijis, and some photo credits and external links are still being confirmed.
